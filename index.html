HakolPrompter

A teleprompter for live presenters · by Hakol Sound

HakolPrompter runs the script for speakers at conferences, ceremonies and live events. It works on macOS and Windows, reads Hebrew and English (right-to-left and mixed), and drives every screen at once: the presenter's monitor, a second display, tablets and phones in a browser, and NDI.

Scripts from Google Docs

The script can come straight from a Google Doc and follow it as the content team edits. Changes never reach the speaker's screen on their own: the operator sees what changed, reviews it, and accepts it, and can freeze the script for the show.

What HakolPrompter can see in your Google account: only the documents you choose in Google's own file picker, and the copy of a script the app makes for the show. Nothing else in your Drive. HakolPrompter has no servers of its own: your documents go between your computer and Google, and nowhere else. Details are in the privacy policy.
